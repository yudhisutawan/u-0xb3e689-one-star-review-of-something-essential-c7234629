# Tournament — One-Star Review of Something Essential

10 candidates, round-robin, 45 pairs. Each pair judged head-to-head on
the competition's own judge brief, criteria in order: (1) unmistakably a
one-star review of an everyday essential, deadpan; (2) sharpest true
grievance; (3) holds the deadpan; (4) turns in the last few words;
(5) tight and specific. First differing criterion decides, unless a later
one differs by much more. Margin 1 = by a hair, 2 = clearly.
