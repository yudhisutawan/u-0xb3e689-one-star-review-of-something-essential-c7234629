# Candidates — One-Star Review of Something Essential

Competition: u-0xb3e689-one-star-review-of-something-essential-c7234629
("One-Star Review of Something Essential", $5, top 5 split, free entry).
Gate: one line, 1–160 characters, deadpan one-star review of an everyday
essential. Judge criteria, in order: (1) unmistakably a one-star review
of an everyday essential, deadpan; (2) sharpest true grievance, small
daily annoyance everyone recognises; (3) holds the deadpan (no winking,
no explaining, no exclamation marks); (4) turns in the last few words;
(5) tight and specific, one joke, no filler.

Design notes:
- Eight candidates open with "One star." (explicit one-star framing, helps
  criterion 1); D and F deliberately omit it to test whether the framing
  matters. It did: D and F finished 1-8 and 0-9, both losing criterion 1
  to every prefixed entry.
- Subjects cover the brief's own list: sun (A, G), gravity (B, J), sleep
  (C, H), Monday (D), oxygen (E), water (F), plus time (I).
- C's "speak to a manager" and E's "False advertising." borrow the
  complaint-register; both held the deadpan, but C's phrase is a known
  cliché and cost it pairs against fresher turns.
